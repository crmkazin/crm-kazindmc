Founded in 2010, Thaver Consultancy Services is the pioneer of organised recruitment services in India. Over the years, we have acted as preferred talent acquisition partner to multinationals and leading Indian businesses to emerge as the leading recruitment brand nationally. This combined with our role as trusted consultants for Indian professionals translates into our core capability - Building Careers. Building Organizations.

Thaver Consultancy Services roots in management consulting enable us to bring a unique approach to recruitment at the middle and senior management levels.

We recruit across various industry segments for multinational corporations as well as leading Indian business houses and have helped shape the careers of over 15,000 professionals over our 10 years history.

Thaver Consultancy Services is proud of its long standing relationships in the industry and the fact the 75% of our business is in the form of repeat business from existing clients is testimony to the quality of work we do.
